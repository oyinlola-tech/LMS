You need an account to use protected LearnBridge features. Your account may operate as a learner, tutor, admin, or superadmin depending on permissions granted by the platform. You are responsible for protecting your login credentials and for activity performed through your account.

2. Courses, Lessons, Assignments, and Grades

Enrollment gives you a personal, limited, non-transferable right to access course materials through LearnBridge. Course content, assignments, quizzes, lesson resources, and downloadable materials may not be resold, redistributed, scraped, or republished unless a tutor or LearnBridge gives written permission.

  • Learners must submit original work and may not abuse upload, quiz, grading, or certificate workflows.
  • Tutors are responsible for course accuracy, fair grading, timely feedback, and lawful use of uploaded learning materials.
  • Admins may review course and assignment records to support moderation, disputes, audits, and platform operations.

3. Certificates and Public Verification

When a learner completes a course, LearnBridge may issue a certificate with a unique certificate ID and public verification page. Learners may share certificates to LinkedIn, download certificate PNG/PDF assets, and embed the credential in an external portfolio.

You may share your own valid certificate. You may not forge, alter, impersonate, sell, or misrepresent a LearnBridge certificate or verification page.
