Privacy Policy

How LearnBridge handles learning, identity, and credential data.

LearnBridge is a full learning platform for students, tutors, admins, mentorship, billing, messages, portfolios, and verifiable certificates. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how your data moves through the product.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Data We Collect Learning Records Certificates Community Billing Security Your Rights

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information needed to run a trustworthy LMS: account details, authentication events, learner progress, course activity, assignment submissions, certificates, profile and portfolio information, support requests, billing status, mentorship applications, and communication records.

Account

Name, email, role, avatar, interests, password hash, verification state, login metadata.

Learning

Enrollments, lesson progress, quiz attempts, notes, bookmarks, grades, certificates, streaks.

Teaching

Tutor profile, courses, modules, lessons, assignments, rubrics, office hours, earnings.

Operations

Admin notes, audit logs, reports, warnings, support tickets, email logs, platform settings.

2. How Learning Data Is Used

Learning data powers the student dashboard, resume-learning flow, recommendations, gradebook, progress reports, achievements, and certificate eligibility. Tutors can see relevant learner progress inside their own courses so they can review submissions, provide feedback, and support students.

  • Progress events help calculate completion percentages and mastery summaries.
  • Assignments and quiz records support grading, feedback, attempts, and audit history.
  • Bookmarks, notes, wishlist items, and study-planner information personalize your experience.

3. Public Certificates and Verification

When you complete a course and receive a certificate, LearnBridge creates a public verification page for that credential. This is intentional: employers, LinkedIn visitors, portfolio viewers, and recruiters need a way to confirm that the certificate is real.

A public certificate verification page may show your name, course title, issue date, certificate ID, LearnBridge branding, downloadable certificate preview, and verification status.

Certificate pages may include social sharing metadata for LinkedIn and portfolio embeds. You control where you share those links, but anyone with the verification URL can view the public credential details.

4. Messages, Discussions, Portfolios, and Community Content

LearnBridge includes direct messages, public discussions, groups, comments, portfolio pages, reviews, mentorship applications, and support conversations.

  • Direct messages are treated as private user communications and protected in transit.
  • Public discussions, comments, reviews, and portfolio pages are visible to their intended audience and may be indexed or moderated.
  • User-generated content may be scanned for abuse, spam, unsafe files, harassment, or policy violations.
  • Files you upload for assignments, messages, portfolios, or course resources are stored so the relevant workflow can function.

5. Billing, Payments, and Subscriptions

We use payment providers to process purchases, subscriptions, course checkout, payout-related workflows, and billing history. LearnBridge stores payment records, subscription state, transaction references, and invoice metadata, but sensitive card processing is handled by payment infrastructure.

6. Security, Admin Access, and Retention

We use authentication, role-based access, rate limiting, audit logs, secure password hashing, file limits, and admin workflows to protect the platform. Admins and superadmins may access operational records when needed for support, moderation, security, billing, compliance, or platform maintenance.

We keep records while your account is active or while they are needed for legitimate platform purposes, including certificate verification, financial records, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and learning history.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can update account details, notification preferences, cookie settings, profile information, and many public-facing profile/portfolio details from the application. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data, subject to limits for certificates, legal records, billing history, abuse prevention, and security logs.

For privacy or security questions, contact the platform operator using the security contact configured for this deployment.
